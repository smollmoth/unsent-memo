Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Overview

Unsent Memo is a space for anonymous professional confessions. We take your privacy seriously. This page explains what we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

What We Collect

  • The text of memos you submit (published anonymously)
  • The recipient label and company name you provide
  • Basic usage data (page views, no personal identifiers)

What We Don't Collect

  • Your name or email address
  • Your IP address stored against your submission
  • Any account information — there are no accounts

How Your Memo Is Used

Submissions are reviewed before publishing. Once approved, your memo appears on the homepage anonymously. We do not sell, share, or monetise your submissions in any way.

Cookies

This site uses no tracking cookies. We do not use Google Analytics or any third-party analytics that identify individual visitors.

Data Retention

Published memos are stored indefinitely unless you request removal. To request that a memo be taken down, contact us below.

Contact

Questions about this policy or removal requests: hello@unsentmemo.com
